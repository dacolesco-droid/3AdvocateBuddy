# How CaseReady Works in Your Organization

**A Practical Guide for Nonprofit Partners**

---

## 🎯 The Problem We Solve

Your intake staff is bottlenecked. When a client calls about an eviction, they've often got:
- A shoebox of scattered notices, receipts, and documents
- A fuzzy timeline ("I think it was last month... or maybe March?")
- No idea what documents they actually have vs. what they need
- High stress and anxiety that makes communication harder

Your staff then spends the first **20-30 minutes** of every call just untangling this mess before they can even assess the legal issue.

**CaseReady changes that.**

---

## 🔄 The User Journey: 4 Simple Steps

### Step 1: The Trigger (Before the Call)
**Who:** Your intake staff, a housing counselor, or a community partner  
**When:** When a client first reaches out for help  

A staff member says:
> *"We can help you with this. Before you call Neighborhood Legal Services for your intake appointment, I want you to use this free tool called CaseReady. It will help you gather your facts and documents so you don't get overwhelmed on the phone."*

They give the client a link (or QR code) to the CaseReady web app.

---

### Step 2: Self-Guided Preparation (At Home)
**Who:** The client (on their own phone or laptop)  
**When:** Before their scheduled intake call  

The client opens the CaseReady app and:
- Sees a clear disclaimer: *"We are an organizational tool, not a law firm. We do not provide legal advice."*
- Answers simple, plain-English prompts:
  - *"What is your current housing status?"*
  - *"Add key events to your timeline."*
  - *"Check off the documents you have in front of you."*
  - *"What are your top 3 questions for a legal advisor?"*
- Can **save and resume** if they get interrupted (e.g., their child needs attention)

**Time required:** 10-15 minutes

---

### Step 3: The Export (The "Aha!" Moment)
**Who:** The client  
**When:** After completing the preparation  

The client clicks **"Generate Intake Packet"** and gets:
- A clean, professional, 1-2 page PDF summary
- A chronological timeline of events
- A checklist of documents they have (and what's missing)
- Their top questions for a legal advisor
- A list of vetted local resources (like your organization and NLSA)

They can print it, email it to themselves, or save the PDF to their phone.

---

### Step 4: The Intake Call/Meeting (The Value Realized)
**Who:** The client and your intake staff (or NLSA staff)  
**When:** During the scheduled intake appointment  

**Without CaseReady:**
- Intake worker: *"Wait, when did you get the notice? Do you have the lease? What does it say? Do you have proof of income?"*
- Client: *"Um, I think it was last month... let me look through this pile of papers..."*
- **Result:** 20-30 minutes of fact-finding before legal assessment

**With CaseReady:**
- Client: *"I have my CaseReady packet right here."*
- Intake worker: *"Perfect. I see your timeline, I see you have the lease and the 10-day notice, and I see your rental assistance was approved. Let's skip the basics and talk about your legal options."*
- **Result:** 5-minute review, then straight to legal assessment

## 🏢 Alternative Scenario: In-Office Triage

Sometimes a client shows up to your office completely unprepared, holding a crumpled shoebox of papers.

**Your staff can say:**
> *"Let's take 10 minutes right now. Open this link on your phone. We're going to fill out this CaseReady form together."*

Even in this scenario, the app is valuable because it **structures the conversation**. Instead of your staff member typing frantically into a blank intake form, the app forces a logical flow (Timeline → Documents → Questions), ensuring no critical detail is missed.

## ❓ Common Questions

### "What if the client doesn't have a smartphone?"
CaseReady is a web app that works on any device with a browser—smartphone, tablet, laptop, or even a library computer. If they don't have access to any device, your staff can use it with them on a shared computer in your office.

### "How much training does my staff need?"
**Zero training required.** Your staff doesn't need to learn a new software system. They just receive a well-formatted PDF or web link from the client, exactly as they would receive a resume or a filled-out form. The app is designed for the *client*, not your staff.

### "What if the client makes a mistake or leaves something out?"
The app allows the client to **save, resume, and edit** their case at any time. They can correct errors, add missing documents, or update their timeline before generating the final packet.

### "Is this legal advice?"
**No.** CaseReady is an organizational tool, not a law firm. We do not provide legal advice, strategy, or representation. Using this tool does not create an attorney-client relationship. The client should always consult with a licensed attorney for legal advice.

### "What happens to the client's data?"
All data is encrypted and stored securely. We do not sell client information, and we do not share case content with sponsors or third parties without explicit consent. The client controls their data and can delete it at any time.

### "How does this integrate with our existing intake process?"
It doesn't replace your intake process—it **enhances** it. The client uses CaseReady *before* they call you. When they do call, they reference their prepared packet, and your staff can focus on legal assessment instead of data entry.
